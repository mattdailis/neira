Distance: 1400m Conditions: Race order: 3V/4V, 2V, 1V. All events saw sustained 10-12 m.p.h. cross-tailwinds, with racing heading inland with the rising tide. Intermittent rollers plagued the first 250m, with conditions flattening out as races approached the 300m. Throughout the day racing conditions trended calmer, going from rougher chop at the start to mild agitation by the end. Comments: We also ran an exhibition heat before the girls' events of a GCDS Men's 1V4+ and 1N4+, listed under the "Fourth Boat" category.
