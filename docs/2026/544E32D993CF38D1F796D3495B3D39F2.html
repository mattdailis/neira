Distance: 1500m Conditions: 10-12 mph wind that varied between a cross wind and a cross tail wind. Conditions in the first 500 and last 250 were quite good, but the body of the race was rowed in challenging waves running perpendicular to the course. Some variation throughout the day so comparisons between races might not be meaningful. Comments: A beautiful afternoon of racing. A few racing notes: St. Marks G1 rowed through a crab in the first few strokes. The Nobles G2 coxswain was forced to steer with hands directly on the cables after losing the steering arm in warm up. Thank you crews and coxswains for your great cooperation on the water. A special thanks to the Nobles coaching team for handling the warm up area so efficiently! Looking forward to some more races in a week. None
